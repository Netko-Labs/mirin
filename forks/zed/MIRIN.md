# mirin's fork of upstream GPUI

The GPUI crates of the zed repository (Zed Industries, Apache-2.0) at
commit `f8cfc7ed` (`../README.md` says what is here and how to re-sync),
forked in the monorepo so `crates/mirin-metal` can build on upstream GPUI
(`crates/mirin-metal/next/Cargo.toml`) beside the fork of gpui 0.2.2
(`../gpui`) until it replaces it (`docs/metal/gpui-next.md`). Every change is
listed here and marked with a `// mirin:` comment in the source. Rows that
port a change of `../gpui` say which (`../gpui/MIRIN.md`).

| The mask map: a frame keeps every content mask as given (`MaskNode`, `Frame::content_masks`, `hitbox_masks`, `content_masks_index` in the prepaint and paint indices; `Window::push_mask_node`, `own_mask`, `copy_masks`; `elements/retained/masks.rs`: `MaskMap`), and every paint operation the mask it was drawn under; a border drawn in strips keeps its strip (`Scene::insert_strip`, `PaintOperation::Strip`, `Placing`) | `crates/gpui/src/window.rs`, `crates/gpui/src/scene.rs`, `crates/gpui/src/elements/retained/{masks,recording,plan,parked}.rs`, `crates/gpui/src/elements/retained.rs` | Port of `../gpui`'s mask-map row (round 6). A primitive or hitbox carried its mask already intersected with the ones around it, so a retained subtree whose own masks (an `overflow: hidden` box, and every border: upstream paints a border on its own quad, in strips each under the content mask intersected with its strip) its view's edge clipped could not be moved, and was drawn again on every scrolled frame (the stress bench's two edge rows: 50 cards a frame). A copied drawing now pushes the masks pushed inside it again, moved, under the mask around it where it is copied, and each primitive, strip, layer and hitbox is clipped by its mask as that clips there, which is what drawing it there gives (at a power of two; elsewhere a mask's size is summed in logical pixels by whoever pushes it, so a drawing with masks pushed inside is not moved: `Recorded::masks`). The `strict` condition is gone; an underline with exclusions pins its subtree. Also fixed: a moved copy dropped a layer that ended up empty, two operations fewer than the drawing it copied, so the ranges of the drawings inside it no longer named theirs (a row then copied another's primitives and hitboxes); an empty layer is now kept as a marker. That is likely what round 5's attempt at moving clipped border strips hit (`layout-check scroll` drew differently, and a press then hit another card). Upstreamable in spirit. |
| Moves on the device grid at any scale factor (`elements/retained/grid.rs`: `Grid`, `cover_device`; `Window::layout_bounds` divides once; `Steadiness::settled_floor`, `on_grid`) | `crates/gpui/src/elements/retained/{grid,plan,recording,steady}.rs`, `crates/gpui/src/window.rs` (`layout_bounds`, `cover_bounds`, `reuse_prepaint_moved`, `reuse_paint_moved`) | Port in spirit of `../gpui`'s `Grid` row (round 6). Where scaling is not exact, a device position within 1/256 of a whole pixel is that pixel before it is floored or ceiled (every mask and shadow, retained or not), a node's origin is its device position and the element offset divided once, and a logical position within 1/512 of a whole device pixel is moved through it (one off it keeps its subtree from moving along that axis); a drawing with masks pushed inside does not move there (their sizes are summed in logical pixels by whoever pushes them). At a power of two nothing changes. Upstreamable in spirit. |

Fork changes upstream has in its own way (nothing to port; see
`docs/metal/gpui-next.md` for the evidence): layer-shell surfaces and the
screen they open on, typed float literals, idle windows that stop
committing, the loop outliving its last window (`QuitMode::Explicit`),
text input only while a field takes text, the toplevel's suspended state
(as window visibility), a layout reused only for the same truncation
width, a line cut from a copy of its runs, no-break spaces and quotes
(UAX #14), `DispatchEventResult` public, and scroll views painted at whole
device pixels (`Window::layout_bounds` snaps the element offset; always on,
so `MIRIN_METAL_SCROLL_SNAP=0` has no effect on this build).

To update: see `../README.md` (merge the `forks-upstream-next` branch), then
reconcile the changes above.

Checks: mirin-metal's tests on this fork, `cd crates/mirin-metal/next &&
cargo test --lib` (the shared sources, with `--features gpui-next` by
default), and `cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings` there.
