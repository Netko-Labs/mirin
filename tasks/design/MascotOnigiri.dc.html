The move
Nori normally wraps the bottom of an onigiri. Here it also reads as a macOS title bar, so the mascot is a window and a rice ball with no compromise in either direction.
Why it works: strongest silhouette of any option here — a triangle with a dark band reads at 16px, in one color, embroidered on a hat. The face has room for expressions.
Tradeoff: onigiri is the most-used Japanese food shape in software branding. Distinctiveness has to come from the title-bar treatment, not the rice ball.
