The logo is Kome

Not a mark with a mascot beside it — the character is the logo. A grain of rice filled with amber to the brew line, with a face. Warmth is the whole point of choosing an opinionated voice; an abstract shape would have thrown it away.

Three cuts — the face survives by getting bolder, not smaller
Full
48px and up
Kōji spores, crease, meniscus, catchlights in the eyes.
Simple
24 – 48px
Spores and crease drop. Eyes grow ~15% to hold at size.
Micro
16 – 24px
Solid amber, two knocked-out eyes, no mouth. Still a face.
Construction
Eyes sit on the 56% line, the brew fills from 64%, and the smile crosses it. Eyes are always above the amber — a face underwater reads as drowning, not brewing.
Clear space & lockup
Clear space is half the logo's height on every side. In the horizontal lockup the wordmark sits at cap-height, one ½h gap to the right, baseline aligned to the brew line.

Logo expression is fixed. The logo always wears the neutral, slightly pleased face above — that is the identity. Kome's other expressions (building, shipped, failed, sleeping) belong to the CLI, empty states and release posts, and are never substituted into a logo slot.
