Kome — refined

A grain, mid-brew

Mirin is not rice. It is rice that has been turned into something — steamed mochigome, inoculated with kōji, brewed into sweet amber wine. So the grain fills with amber from the bottom, and the kōji bloom sits on its shoulder. The transformation is the logo.

Rice body. Warm off-white, with the crease every grain has.
Amber level. The wine. Doubles as a progress fill.
Kōji bloom. Three spores. The step that makes it mirin.
Face. Sits above the amber line, never inside it.
Expressions — the amber level carries the state
idle
building
shipped
failed
sleeping
The idea worth keeping: the amber level is not decoration, it is a value. Empty grain at 0%, full at 100%, red when the build breaks. The mascot and the progress indicator are the same object.
