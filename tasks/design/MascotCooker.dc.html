Rice concept 04 — Cooker

Suihanki

A rice cooker is a machine you put raw material into and get a finished thing out of, with one button. That is the framework, not a metaphor for it — and its control panel is a little screen with a face in it.

The move
The panel is a screen, so the whole face is state: eyes for idle, a progress bar while building, red crosses on failure, steam when mirin dev is hot. The CLI has a mascot for free.
Why it works: the only concept that is about what the tool DOES, not just where the name comes from. An appliance with a screen is a build tool with a status.
Tradeoff: the busiest silhouette of the four — lid, body, panel, steam. At 16px it collapses to a rounded box and loses the joke.
