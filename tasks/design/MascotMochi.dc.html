Rice concept 02 — Mochi

Mochi

Pounded glutinous rice — mochigome, the exact rice mirin is brewed from. A soft blob that squashes and stretches, which is the most animatable shape on this page.

The move
Squash and stretch is free personality: mochi compresses on a button press, wobbles while a build runs, flattens on an error. The blob is the animation system.
Why it works: the most literal rice tie of the four — mochigome is the actual ingredient. Soft, warm, and unusually expressive for a systems tool.
Tradeoff: a rounded blob is close to a generic mascot blob, and it says nothing about desktop. Without the face it is just an oval.
