Page 1 — Directions
A · Amber Izakaya
Leans on the name. 味醂 is sweet amber rice wine — warm near-black, editorial serif, craft-kitchen warmth. Reads human and memorable in a category that all looks the same.
Tradeoff: warmth can read "food blog" rather than "systems tool". Nothing in the code is amber today.
B · Native Glass
Extends what already ships. Indigo #6d6df0 and translucent white cards are literally in examples/spotlight and liquid-glass today. The site and the app match because they are the same palette.
Tradeoff: indigo-on-dark is the default dev-tool look. Differentiation has to come from the real app captures.
C · Paper Terminal
Light paper, black ink, one hot accent, mono everywhere, visible grid rules. The only light direction here. Says "this is a tool, read the docs" and refuses to sell.
Tradeoff: a dark app capture sitting on light paper needs careful framing or it looks pasted on.
