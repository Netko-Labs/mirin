Mascot concept — Pane

A window that woke up

The differentiator is the transparent window with native material behind it. So the mascot is that window — translucent, layered, and it shows what is behind it.

silhouette @ 16px
app icon lockup
error / 404 pose
Why it works: the translucency is the product. Whatever sits behind the mascot shows through it, so the logo itself demonstrates the one thing Electron cannot do casually.
Tradeoff: translucency dies at favicon size and in single-color contexts — it needs a solid fallback mark, which is half a second logo. Also the least warm of the three.
