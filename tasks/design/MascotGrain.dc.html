The move
One grain is a mascot; many grains are a system. Loading states scatter them, the platform matrix uses them as row markers, a filled grain is a progress unit.
Why it works: the only concept here that is a real logo as well as a mascot. Scales to a favicon, stamps into a CLI splash, tiles into a pattern.
Tradeoff: at small sizes a lone grain reads as a bean, an egg, or a pill. It needs the three-grain mark alongside it to disambiguate.
