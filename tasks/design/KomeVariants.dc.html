Kome — treatments

How much wine in the rice

Same grain, four points along the brew. Left is raw rice, right is finished mirin in the cup. Pick where on that line the mark should sit.

1 · Kōji
Raw grain, spores blooming. Cleanest silhouette, warmest read, but the wine is only implied.
2 · Half-brewed pick
Rice above, wine below. Both halves of the name in one shape, and the level is a live value.
3 · Poured
Fully mirin. Boldest at any size and the best favicon, but with the crease gone it is a drop, not a grain.
4 · Ochoko
The grain in a cup of its own mirin. Most story, most charm, worst at small sizes — an illustration, not a mark.
My pick: 2 for the mark, 4 for the illustration. Half-brewed carries rice and wine at once and turns the logo into a status indicator. Ochoko is the character you use on the 404 page and in release posts, where it has room to breathe.
1 and 3 are the same mark at opposite ends of the brew — if you want one flat colour forever, 3 is the honest choice and 2 is overreach.
