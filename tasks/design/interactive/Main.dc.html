Five explainers — all live, all pokeable

Show the mechanism, don't describe it.

Mirin's three hard-to-explain ideas are all motion — a call crossing a process boundary, a window being described rather than constructed, a check driving an app you cannot see. Prose loses to a thing you can click.

Motion budget: every animation here is triggered by the reader, never on scroll and never on a loop. Nothing moves until someone asks it to — docs that twitch while you read them are worse than static ones.
All five degrade: each is a real component with a static first frame, so a reader with reduced motion or no JS still gets the diagram.
